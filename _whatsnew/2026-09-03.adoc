=== AI agents for Workload Factory

Meet your AI assistant for storage operations. AI agents use your Workload Factory credentials to provide insights about your environment, helping you identify risks, improve workloads, and troubleshoot issues without leaving Workload Factory.

Access AI agents through the new Explore Agents section in **Ask Me**. By default, the agents work in read-only mode and provide recommendations and analysis. You can also enable read-write mode in Agents settings to allow the agents to take actions for you. Every action requires your approval before it runs.

Use **Agents settings** to choose which credentials the agents can access, add custom instructions, and adjust response detail levels.

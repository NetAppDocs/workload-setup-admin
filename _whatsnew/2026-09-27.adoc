=== Customer-managed Bedrock for AI features

Workload Factory now supports customer-managed AWS Bedrock for AI features to let your organization use its own model.

On the AI Settings page, account administrators can connect to customer-managed Bedrock, select the AWS credentials linked to the Workload Factory IAM role, and choose the model or inference profile to use.

After you connect, Workload Factory lets you know whether the connection was successful.

link:https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/workload-setup-admin/manage-genai-features.html[Learn about managing AI features]

=== Customer-managed Bedrock support for classification

Workload Factory now lets customers use their own Amazon Bedrock setup for workload tagging classification. Customers can provide their own Bedrock endpoint and credentials so classification requests run through their own Bedrock configuration.
